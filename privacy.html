Accutech Calibrations: QuickBooks Integration Privacy Policy

Accutech Calibrations ("we") runs a private, internal integration that connects only to Accutech Calibrations' own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to other businesses or the public.

Data accessed. Accounting data in our own QuickBooks Online company (company info, accounts, customers, vendors, invoices, payments, and journal entries), through Intuit's official API.

Use. Only for our own bookkeeping: reading balances and posting entries we have reviewed. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties.

Storage and security. Access credentials are stored securely with restricted access and are never put in source code. All traffic uses HTTPS.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time in QuickBooks Online (Settings > Apps > Connected apps), which immediately ends the integration's access.
